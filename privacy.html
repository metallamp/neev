Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Neev ("we", "our", "the site") is a free fitness planning tool available at ne-ev.in. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Neev does not require you to create an account or provide your email address. Your fitness survey responses are stored only in your browser's local storage and are never sent to our servers.

Data We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate the site:

  • Survey responses — height, weight, age, goal, activity level, food preference. Stored locally in your browser only. Cleared when you clear your browser data.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, device type. Collected anonymously via Google Analytics to help us improve the site.
  • Cookies — used by Google Analytics and Google AdSense (see below). No personal cookies are set by Neev itself.

Google AdSense & Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Affiliate Links

Some links on this site — particularly in supplement recommendations — may be affiliate links. This means we may earn a small commission if you purchase through those links, at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products we genuinely believe in.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — anonymous site usage tracking. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — advertising network. Google Ads Policy
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google servers. Google may log your IP address.
  • Netlify — web hosting provider. May collect server access logs.

Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics uses these to understand how visitors interact with the site.
  • Advertising cookies — Google AdSense uses these to show relevant ads.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

Children's Privacy

Neev is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through this site, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data directly, there is no personal profile to access, update, or delete. If you have any concerns about data collected by third-party services (Google Analytics, AdSense), you can manage those through your Google account settings or browser cookie controls.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site grows. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
